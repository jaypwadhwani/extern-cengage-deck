Extern × Cengage03

Phase 1 Spring 2027

How students get in

1

Instructor picks

The professor teaching the course chooses which students take part.

2

List comes to us

Names and emails. No systems have to talk to each other.

3

We open accounts

Created ahead of time, with full access for the length of the program.

4

Student registers

Signs up once with that email. No payment step.

The student list is the gate, not the link. A course link opens the door but grants nothing, so it cannot be passed to someone without a seat.

Students see the whole catalog. The instructor recommends two to four programs that fit the course, and an Extern program manager holds the cohort to them. That combination is what drives 80%+ completion at our university partners.

Phase 1 Program metadata

The catalog, as data

Available for the pilot. A read-only endpoint returning currently active programs, in this shape, if you would rather ingest the catalog than read it.

One active program

{
  "title": "Data Analytics Externship",
  "company": "Breaking Games",
  "category": "Data & Analytics",
  "skills": ["SQL", "Visualisation"],
  "start_date": "2027-01-19",
  "end_date": "2027-03-30",
  "weeks": 10,
  "time_commitment": "5-7 hrs/week",
  "updated_at": "2026-09-14"
}

Three things to note

  • start_date end_date Each run has fixed dates, and runs open continuously. So a recommendation can be timed to a course.
  • updated_at Pull only what changed. No full refetch.
  • competencies The next field. A matrix in Phase 1, live data in Phase 2.
Extern × Cengage06

Phase 2 The longer-term goal

Put the externship inside MindTap

Allie’s point on the call: students already go from the LMS into MindTap, and Extern makes it a third platform. Phase 2 removes that hop by embedding the externship in your page, so the student never leaves Cengage.

Today
LMSLearning Management System → MindTap → Extern leaves your platform
Phase 2
LMSLearning Management System → MindTap Extern, embedded

Canvas, Blackboard, or whichever platform the school already runs. Students pass through it before reaching MindTap.

Shared sign-in

Students arrive already authenticated through your identity provider, with no separate Extern password.

Catalog feed

So the right program appears in the right course, chosen by your engine rather than by browsing.

Enrolment

Your system places a named student into a named program directly. This is what replaces the student list.

Skills data back

What the student demonstrated returns to your engine, closing the loop you described.

Extern × Cengage07

Why this order

Pilot first, then integrate

  1. 01

    Build before proof and you build on guesses

    A spring cohort tells us which programs instructors pick and where students get stuck.

  2. 02

    Where it lives decides how they log in

    Your point, Vijay. Embedded or linked changes how authentication has to work.

  3. 03

    Nothing gets thrown away

    Phase 1 produces the same data Phase 2 automates. Same journey, people instead of systems.
